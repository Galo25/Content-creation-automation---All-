# Marketing Executive Summary
## ACA & Medicare Advertising Opportunity — 2026

**Prepared:** May 2026 | **Vertical:** Health Insurance — Senior & Pre-Medicare Market
**Distribution:** RevContent · Taboola · Outbrain · MGID · Google Display

---

## 1. Market Opportunity

The 2026 health insurance market presents one of the most significant native advertising opportunities in the senior segment. Two converging factors make this a high-urgency window:

- **Medicare Advantage enrollment** continues to grow — over **32 million Americans** are now enrolled in MA plans, representing more than 50% of all Medicare eligibles
- **ACA subsidy restructuring** in 2026 has reinstated the subsidy cliff at 400% FPL, creating millions of newly price-sensitive consumers who must re-shop their coverage

Together, these dynamics are driving massive search and intent volume around plan comparison, cost reduction, and eligibility — the exact triggers that native advertising converts best.

## 2. Pricing Intelligence — The Core Message

### ACA Marketplace (Adults 50–64, Pre-Medicare)

| State | Lowest Bronze Premium | After Subsidies (~$25K income) |
|---|---|---|
| New Hampshire | **$0 / mo** | Lowest benchmark nationally |
| Maryland | **$0 / mo** | Lowest Bronze & Gold nationally |
| Georgia | ~$23 / mo | Affordable rural options |
| Texas | ~$31 / mo | Varies significantly by county |
| Florida | ~$38 / mo | +30% premium spike in 2026 |

**Key insight:** Tax credits cover an average of **91% of the lowest-cost plan premium** for eligible enrollees. For incomes between 100–150% FPL (~$15,650–$23,475/yr for a single adult), the subsidy is 100% — meaning a **$0 premium Bronze plan is a real, deliverable offer** in most markets.

**Subsidy eligibility:** Single adults earning up to **$62,600/yr** qualify for some level of subsidy. Families of four qualify up to **$128,600/yr**. This is an enormous addressable audience.

### Medicare Advantage (Seniors 65+)

| Metric | 2026 Figure |
|---|---|
| Seniors with access to a $0-premium MA plan | **98%** |
| Average monthly MA premium (all enrollees) | **$14/mo** |
| Standard Part B premium (Original Medicare) | $202.90/mo |
| Implied monthly savings for switchers | **Up to $188/mo · $2,435/yr** |
| Grocery/flex card allowance (chronic condition) | $25–$150/mo |

**Key insight:** The $202.90 → $0 price delta is the single most powerful message in the Medicare advertising space. It is factually accurate, immediately tangible, and triggers loss aversion — the highest-converting psychological hook in insurance native ads.

## 3. Target Audience Profiles

### Audience A — Medicare Seniors (Priority)
- **Age:** 65–80
- **Income:** Fixed / retirement income
- **Pain point:** Paying $150–$250/mo for Medicare they don't fully understand
- **Trigger:** "I didn't know I could pay less"
- **Platforms:** RevContent (AOL, Newsbreak, NY Post), Taboola (Fox News, MSN)
- **Best creative hook:** Loss aversion — "$202/mo → $0/mo" price comparison

### Audience B — Chronic Condition Seniors (High Value)
- **Age:** 65–78, Diabetes / Heart Disease / COPD diagnosis
- **Pain point:** High out-of-pocket costs for prescriptions, specialists, groceries
- **Trigger:** "Up to $150/month grocery card just for having Medicare"
- **Platforms:** MGID, RevContent, Facebook 65+ health interest
- **Best creative hook:** Tangible benefit stacking — Dental + Vision + Grocery Card

### Audience C — Pre-Medicare Adults (Growth Segment)
- **Age:** 50–64, uninsured or underinsured
- **Income:** $20,000–$62,600/yr (subsidy-eligible)
- **Pain point:** Paying $300–$600/mo for individual coverage or going uninsured
- **Trigger:** "My state has plans from $0/month"
- **Platforms:** Taboola (Today.com, USA Today), Outbrain (premium news), Google Display
- **Best creative hook:** Geographic price anchor — state-by-state premium table

## 4. Creative Strategy & Performance Benchmarks

### Psychological Hooks Ranked by Expected CTR

| Hook Type | Example | Est. CTR (Native) |
|---|---|---|
| FOMO benefit stack | "Your Medicare Is Missing 3 Things" | **1.0–1.8%** |
| Geographic specificity | "NH Residents: Plans From $0/Month" | 0.9–1.6% |
| Loss aversion | "Still Paying $202/mo for Medicare?" | 0.8–1.4% |
| Authority + social proof | "98% of Seniors Qualify — Do You?" | 0.7–1.2% |
| Condition-specific | "COPD? Your Inhaler May Be Covered Free" | 0.9–1.5% |

### Ad Format Recommendations

| Format | Size | Best Platform | Use Case |
|---|---|---|---|
| Native square | 600×600 | RevContent, Taboola | Primary feed unit |
| Landscape banner | 1200×628 | Outbrain, Facebook | High-reach awareness |
| Rectangle | 300×250 | Google Display, MGID | Retargeting |
| Leaderboard | 728×90 | Desktop news sites | Brand reinforcement |

### Copy Principles That Drive Conversions

1. **Specific dollar amounts** — "$202" beats "expensive". "$150/mo grocery card" beats "extra benefits"
2. **Curiosity gap in headline** — "3 Things Your Medicare Is Missing" outperforms direct statements
3. **Geographic relevance** — State-specific copy lifts CTR 20–35% over generic national copy
4. **Zero-friction CTA** — "Check My Eligibility" converts better than "Apply Now" (less commitment)
5. **Trust signals** — "No obligation · Results in 60 seconds · Licensed agents" reduce drop-off

## 6. Funnel Recommendation

For maximum ROI, avoid sending traffic directly to a form. The highest-performing funnel for Medicare/ACA native is:

```
Native Ad (curiosity hook)
    ↓
Advertorial Landing Page (800–1,200 words, educational)
    ↓
Soft CTA → Lead Capture Form
    ↓
Licensed Agent Follow-Up (phone/text within 5 min)
```

Advertorial funnels achieve **5–10% conversion rates** vs. 2–4% for direct-to-form. Leads from advertorial funnels also score **15–20% higher** on quality metrics, reducing agent time-to-close.

## 7. Compliance Snapshot

All Medicare and ACA advertising must adhere to:

- **CMS Marketing Guidelines** — no misleading benefit claims, no implying government endorsement, required disclaimers on all materials
- **TCPA** — explicit written consent required for SMS/phone follow-up leads
- **FTC Native Ad Disclosure** — must be labeled "Sponsored" or "Advertisement"
- **State DOI regulations** — vary by state; agent license disclosure required
- **Platform policies** — RevContent/Taboola prohibit fear-based language about Medicare cuts or benefit loss without factual basis

## 8. Executive Recommendation

**The 2026 Medicare Advantage market is the most cost-efficient senior lead generation opportunity in digital media.** With 98% of beneficiaries having access to a $0-premium plan and average Part B savings of $188/month, the value proposition writes itself. The creative challenge is simply making consumers aware of what they're leaving on the table.

**Top 3 actions to launch:**
1. **Lead with Ad 03** ("Your Medicare Is Missing 3 Things") — highest projected CTR, strongest benefit stack, easiest to A/B test
2. **Run state-specific ACA campaigns** in New Hampshire, Maryland, and Georgia — these markets have the most dramatic price story ($0/mo) and least creative saturation
3. **Build an advertorial funnel** before scaling spend — the $20–$45 native CPL is only achievable with a high-converting mid-funnel page to qualify intent
